About Wesley Ulloa

The broker behind one of Miami's most trusted names.

Miami native, FIU-trained, and founder of LUXE Properties — with nearly three thousand homes sold across South Florida.

Wesley Ulloa · Broker-Owner

The broker behind the brokerage

A Miami native who built one of the city's most trusted names in real estate.

Born and raised in Miami, Wesley Ulloa has spent more than a decade guiding buyers, sellers and investors through South Florida's most competitive market. She earned her Bachelor's in Business Administration and a Master's in International Real Estate from Florida International University, then cut her teeth at Fortune International Realty before founding LUXE Properties in 2014.

Today she leads a brokerage of nearly one hundred agents and has personally helped close more than 2,900 transactions, from first homes in Kendall to eighteen-million-dollar residences on the water. Her approach is unhurried and exacting: the right home, the right terms, and a negotiation handled with discretion.
